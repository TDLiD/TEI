Staff Quick User Guide (English)

1. Attendance Check (Time Clock)

Clock-In: Click the [ATTENDANCE] button when you arrive.
Clock-Out: Click the [LEAVE] button before you go home.
Check Status: Your daily working hours and "Late" status are updated in real-time on the dashboard calendar.

2. Daily Report

Write Report: Select a date on the calendar and click [Write Report].
Contents: Fill in "Today's To-Do" and "Work Summary."
Save: Click [Save Report] to submit. Reports are linked to your attendance data for the day.

3. Leave Request (Vacation)

Request: Go to the [LEAVE] tab and click a date on the calendar.
Details: Choose the leave type (Annual, Sick, etc.) and write the reason.
Auto-Exclude: The system automatically excludes weekends from your request.
Status: You can track if your request is "Pending" or "Approved" on the leave calendar.

4. Shared Schedule

View Events: Go to the [CALENDAR] tab to see team schedules and office events.
Add Event: Click a date to add a personal or shared schedule.
Sync: All staff members see the same master calendar for better coordination.

5. Statistics & Settings

My Stats: View your attendance rate, late count, and average work hours in the [STATS] tab.
Level System: Check your current rank (e.g., Gold, Master) based on your performance points.
Profile: Change your password and update your profile image in the [SETTING] tab.

[Tip]
• Missing Records: If you see a "Missing Records" popup, please fill in the unrecorded times immediately.
• Mobile Friendly: This system is optimized for mobile; you can easily check and report via your smartphone.

Panduan Cepat Penggunaan (Untuk Staf / Indonesian)

1. Cek Kehadiran (Absensi)

Clock-In (Masuk): Klik tombol [ATTENDANCE] saat Anda tiba di kantor.
Clock-Out (Pulang): Klik tombol [LEAVE] sebelum Anda pulang.
Cek Status: Jam kerja harian và status "Terlambat" akan diperbarui otomatis di kalender dashboard.

2. Laporan Harian (Daily Report)

Tulis Laporan: Pilih tanggal di kalender dan klik [Write Report].
Isi Laporan: Lengkapi bagian "Today's To-Do" (Rencana) dan "Work Summary" (Hasil Kerja).
Simpan: Klik [Save Report]. Laporan ini akan terhubung dengan data absensi Anda.

3. Pengajuan Cuti (Leave Request)

Ajukan Cuti: Buka tab [LEAVE] dan klik tanggal pada kalender.
Detail: Pilih tipe cuti (Tahunan, Sakit, dll.) dan tulis alasannya.
Tanpa Akhir Pekan: Sistem akan otomatis menghapus hari Sabtu dan Minggu dari total pengajuan.
Status: Pantau apakah status cuti Anda "Pending" (Menunggu) atau "Approved" (Disetujui) di kalender cuti.

4. Berbagi Jadwal (Shared Schedule)

Lihat Jadwal: Buka tab [CALENDAR] untuk melihat jadwal tim dan acara kantor.
Tambah Jadwal: Klik pada tanggal untuk menambah jadwal pribadi atau bersama.
Sinkronisasi: Semua staf melihat kalender yang sama untuk koordinasi yang lebih baik.

5. Statistik & Pengaturan

Statistik Saya: Lihat tingkat kehadiran, jumlah terlambat, và rata-rata jam kerja di tab [STATS].
Sistem Level: Cek peringkat Anda (contoh: Gold, Master) berdasarkan poin performa kerja.
Profil: Ubah kata sandi (password) và foto profil Anda di tab [SETTING].

[Tips Tambahan]
• Data Kosong: Jika muncul pop-up "Missing Records", segera isi waktu absensi yang terlewat.
• Ramah Ponsel: Sistem ini optimal untuk HP; Anda bisa absen và melapor dengan mudah lewat smartphone.

관리자 퀵 사용 매뉴얼 (Admin Guide - Korean)

1. 대시보드 및 현황 모니터링

전 직원의 출근율, 미처리 휴가, 최근 업무 보고를 한눈에 확인하고 클릭해서 수정 가능합니다. 상단 알림 배너로 승인 대기 건을 즉시 확인합니다.

2. 근태 및 업무 보고 관리

[ATTENDANCE] 메뉴에서 누락된 시간을 직접 수정하고, [REPORT] 메뉴에서 직원들의 일일 보고서를 날짜별로 확인하며 관리자 전용 메모를 남깁니다.

3. 휴가 승인 프로세스

[LEAVE] 메뉴에서 'Pending' 건의 사유를 확인한 후 [Approve] 또는 [Reject] 버튼으로 결재합니다. 승인 시 근태 기록에 자동 반영됩니다.

4. 마스터 스케줄 관리

[CALENDAR]에서 전사 일정을 등록합니다. 드래그 앤 드롭으로 날짜 변경이 가능하며, 보라색 아이콘은 관리자 중요 메모 포함 일정입니다.

5. 통계 및 설정

[STATS]에서 지각 횟수 등 추이를 그래프로 분석하고, [SETTING]에서 직원 추가 및 권한 변경, JSON 파일로 데이터 백업/복구가 가능합니다.

※ 관리자 운영 팁: 보안을 위해 30분 미활동 시 자동 로그아웃됩니다. 엑셀 버튼으로 월간 근태 데이터를 내려받으세요.

Admin Quick User Guide (English)

1. Dashboard & Monitoring

View attendance rates, pending leave requests, and recent work reports at a glance. Alert banners notify you of requests waiting for approval.

2. Attendance & Report Management

Manually enter missing times in [ATTENDANCE] and browse daily reports by date in [REPORT] with admin-only notes.

3. Leave Approval Process

Review "Pending" requests in [LEAVE] and click [Approve] or [Reject]. Approved leave is automatically synced to attendance records.

4. Master Schedule Management

Register company-wide events in [CALENDAR]. Supports drag & drop. Purple icons indicate events with important admin notes.

5. Statistics & System Settings

Analyze late counts and work trends in [STATS]. Manage staff, reset passwords, and export/import JSON backups in [SETTING].

※ Admin Pro-Tips: Auto logout after 30 mins of inactivity. Use the Excel button for monthly data documentation.
